The Story

Synopsis

A virus begins in a remote corner of the world.

Within weeks, it spreads across continents. Borders mean nothing. Distance means nothing. By the time people understand what it is, it is already too late.

More than 99.9% of the population is gone.

What remains is not just an empty world, but a changed one.

Hudson is a thousand miles from home when everything collapses. His family is gone, the rules are gone, and the road ahead is filled with silence and uncertainty. As he makes his way across a broken country, he discovers something unexpected. Survival is not just about finding food and shelter. It is about finding people.

Some are worth saving.

Some are not.

In the ruins of the Midwest, small groups begin to form. Communities built on trust, fear, and power. Leaders rise. Some protect. Others control. And in a world without law, the difference between the two can mean life or death.

This is a story of survival, of rebuilding, and of what people become when everything is taken away.

After the Collapse

The Empty World

Highways stretch through silent towns. Businesses remain stocked for customers who will never return. Power grids fail, governments vanish, and the familiar rules of daily life disappear.

Yet the end of civilization does not end humanity. Scattered survivors begin choosing whether the future will be built through cooperation, fear, compassion, or force.

Those Who Remain

Meet the Survivors

Ordinary people carry the knowledge, failures, loyalties, and regrets of the old world into a future no one expected to see.

The Survivor

Hudson Hollins

Stranded more than a thousand miles from home, Hudson crosses a devastated America carrying the weight of his past and the uncertain hope that someone else may still be alive.

Former Teacher

Stephanie

Before the virus, Stephanie taught children. In the new world, her calm judgment, compassion, and determination help others endure circumstances no classroom could have prepared her for.

Former Military

Omar

Disciplined, observant, and trained to remain steady under pressure, Omar brings valuable experience to a world where danger often arrives without warning.

The Fighter

Shaz

Confident and resilient, Shaz refuses to be defined by what she has lost. Her strength comes from determination, clear judgment, and the refusal to surrender her independence.

Retired Nurse

Mae

Mae spent her working life caring for others. With hospitals gone and trained medical help nearly nonexistent, her nursing knowledge becomes one of the community’s greatest resources.

The Optimist

Eddie

Eddie retains warmth and humor in a world that has given everyone reasons to become colder. His presence helps remind the group what they are trying to preserve.

Loyal Companion

Shadoe

Intelligent, watchful, and dependable, Shadoe becomes Hudson’s companion on the road and a source of trust in a world where trust has become rare.

Two Visions of Survival

The Communities

The survivors do not simply inherit an empty world. They must decide how power, protection, responsibility, and freedom will work within it.

The Walmart Parking Lot

The RV Community

Motorhomes and travel trailers gathered in an abandoned Walmart parking lot become the foundation of a new neighborhood. Survivors share supplies, skills, meals, protection, and the difficult work of building trust.

Tony — A retired man with practical experience and the willingness to work.

Moses — A retired Navy veteran and former car salesman whose steady manner helps calm conflict.

Dwight — A retired ironworker whose reliability and knowledge make him invaluable.

Power Through Fear

Bruce’s Territory

Bruce was a lifelong thief who spent years in prison. When the virus swept through the prison system and order collapsed, he escaped into a world with no laws left to restrain him.

Some people lost everything when civilization fell. Bruce gained everything.

Inside Bruce’s World

Control, Loyalty, and Fear

Bruce’s Men

Mitchell, Jordy, and Sloan

Mitchell is tall, broad, and experienced. Jordy is younger, wiry, and impulsive. Sloan is stocky, quiet, and imposing. Together they help Bruce enforce his control over those around him.

Caught in Bruce’s World

Paula

Young and unhappy, Paula lives beneath Bruce’s control. Her silence does not mean acceptance, and the desire for freedom remains even when escape appears impossible.

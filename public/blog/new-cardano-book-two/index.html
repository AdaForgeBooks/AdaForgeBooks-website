New Cardano Book Two: Fight for a New Beginning

New Cardano Book Two will follow the survivors as they leave the shelter and begin searching for a way to live in the aftermath.

The trio wants to establish a peaceful commune built around cooperation and survival.

At the same time, Mad Marve, also known as the Black Baron, has begun creating his own new society. His plan is a dictatorship where he rules.

Both groups want the same territory, and the two visions cannot survive side by side.

Demise Progress Update and Ada Forge Books Website Improvements

Work has been steadily progressing on my upcoming novel, Demise: A Legacy Undone. The story continues to take shape, and I am currently working toward a late November release.

There has also been quite a bit of work happening behind the scenes here at Ada Forge Books. I have recently been going through the website page by page, correcting issues with the headers and footers, improving navigation, cleaning up links, optimizing images, and making sure the site works properly across its different sections.

With a growing website, there is always a chance that something gets missed. If you come across a broken link, missing image, page that does not display correctly, or any other problem, please let me know.

You can send me an email using the Contact Me information on the website. Reports from readers are appreciated and help keep the site working the way it should.

More information about Demise: A Legacy Undone will be coming as we get closer to November, including additional details about the novel and its release.

Thank you for visiting Ada Forge Books and for following along.

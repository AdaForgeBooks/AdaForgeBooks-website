A Message from Bill Sender

You came back.

I wondered if you would.

There isn't much to see yet. These things take time, and rushing only makes a mess of things.

So be patient.

Go home. Lock your doors. Get some sleep.

Then come back and check again soon.

I'll still be here.

— Bill Sender

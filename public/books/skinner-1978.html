The Story

Synopsis

Some monsters are loud.

Others are quiet.

The dangerous ones are the ones no one notices.

In 1978, Bill is just another employee behind the counter of a small-town fast-food restaurant called Cluck Palace. He works the late shifts, keeps the place clean, and avoids the drama that surrounds most of the other employees. To the people around him, Bill is forgettable.

Quiet.

Reliable.

Invisible.

When Bill suddenly quits his job and takes a position at Redmark Industries, no one thinks much about it. People come and go in factory towns all the time.

But something inside Bill is changing.

What no one knows is that Bill is living with a mind that has begun to betray him. For years, medication kept the voices quiet. Now the medication is gone.

At first, the change is small. Bill talks to himself more often. His thoughts drift into strange directions. He begins to see patterns in ordinary events. Things that once seemed harmless now feel like personal insults.

The world begins to look different through Bill’s eyes.

More hostile.

More cruel.

More deserving of correction.

Then the men at Redmark start disappearing. A worker misses a shift and never returns. Another vanishes from his apartment without explanation. Soon there are rumors spreading across the factory floor. People whisper about arguments, grudges, and secrets buried beneath years of working side by side.

Detectives Marshall Graves and Denning are called in to investigate what first appears to be a routine missing-person case. But as the list of vanished men grows longer, the situation begins to feel less like coincidence and more like something far darker.

All the while, Bill continues showing up for work. He keeps his head down. He listens. He observes. And the voice in his mind grows louder every day.

As Bill slips further away from the man he once was, the line between reality and delusion begins to disappear. His thoughts become darker, his reasoning more distorted, and his actions increasingly dangerous.

To the outside world, he still appears calm. But inside his mind, something else has taken hold. Something that calls itself The Skinner.

Inside Bill’s Mind

Bill Sender

Bill appears ordinary because ordinary is exactly what the world expects him to be. He is quiet, dependable, methodical, and easy to overlook. At Cluck Palace and later at Redmark Industries, he keeps his head down and rarely gives anyone a reason to remember him.

Beneath that calm surface, Bill is losing his ability to separate reality from delusion. The voice he hears does not present itself as frightening. It speaks with patience, certainty, and the promise that it understands the world better than he does.

The most dangerous part of Bill is how normal he still appears.

The People of Galesburg

Meet the Characters

Coworkers, investigators, and familiar faces move through Bill’s quiet world without realizing how quickly the ordinary is becoming something else.

Factory Life

Redmark Industries

Production Supervisor

Gary

Large, intimidating, and rarely seen without a cigar, Gary supervises the production floor through pressure, sharp criticism, and the certainty that every worker should move faster than he does.

Maintenance Worker

Troy

Years of smoking, drinking, and factory work have left Troy looking older than his age. He lives alone, keeps familiar routines, and carries the weariness of a man who expects very little from life.

Quality Assurance Inspector

Kyle

Kyle dislikes his job and puts in only enough effort to survive another shift. Lazy, unmotivated, and always searching for the easiest way through a task, he remains at Redmark for one simple reason: somebody has to do the job.

Redmark Employee

Tyler

Tyler is an experienced employee whose life follows a familiar rhythm between factory shifts and quiet evenings at home. He is known around Redmark as a man who understands the workplace and the people inside it.

Before Redmark

Cluck Palace

Restaurant Manager

Rick

Rick manages Cluck Palace with a practical, no-nonsense style. His worn uniform, stained shirt, and familiar presence reflect years spent running a busy restaurant. Bill was once simply another quiet employee under his supervision.

The Investigation

Galesburg Police Department

Detectives

Denning and Marshall Graves

Marshall Graves is patient, methodical, and unwilling to dismiss small details. Denning brings experience and instinct to their partnership. Together, they begin examining events that no longer feel like isolated disappearances.

Beyond the Factory

Other Characters

Psychiatrist

Dr. Redmond

Dr. Redmond has spent his career studying the human mind and helping patients maintain stability. His work with Bill reveals how fragile that stability can become when treatment ends and isolation takes its place.

Retired Resident

Walt Granger

Frail and in declining health, Walt lives quietly inside his apartment surrounded by familiar possessions and memories. Despite his condition, he remains warm, polite, and grateful for small acts of kindness.

The World of the Novel

Two Familiar Places

One is where Bill learned how easily he could disappear into the background. The other is where his quiet new life begins to change.

Small-Town Fast Food

Cluck Palace

A familiar franchise restaurant filled with glowing menu boards, inexpensive meals, late shifts, and employees who rarely imagine that anything truly unusual could begin there.

Precision Parts to Specifications

Redmark Industries

A weathered brick factory where machinists, inspectors, maintenance workers, and supervisors spend long shifts amid heat, smoke, cutting oil, production quotas, and old grudges.

Invisibility

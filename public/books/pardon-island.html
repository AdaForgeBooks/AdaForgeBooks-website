The Story

Welcome to Mercy Creek

Every season, two hundred fifty strangers arrive on Pardon Island to begin an extraordinary social experiment.

Nestled along a quiet stretch of coastline, the picturesque town of Mercy Creek appears to be the perfect American community. Tree-lined streets, friendly neighbors, family-owned businesses, and a slower pace of life create the illusion that its residents have stepped back into a simpler time.

For one unforgettable season, two hundred fifty people will call Mercy Creek home.

But there is one secret that changes everything.

Unknown to the residents, five of their neighbors are convicted death row inmates who have been given an unprecedented opportunity to live among ordinary people. Their identities are known only to the production team and the television audience. Every other resident believes all two hundred fifty participants earned their place through the same nationwide selection process.

No one in Mercy Creek knows the truth.

As friendships form, romances blossom, rivalries emerge, and trust is tested, one question quietly lingers beneath every interaction:

Would you see someone differently if you knew their past?

Meet This Season's Residents

The Cast of Mercy Creek

These biographies are presented exactly as the television audience would see them in the show's promotional campaign.

Tennessee

Caleb Mercer

A former history teacher and track coach, Caleb is calm under pressure, naturally observant, and slow to judge. He hopes Mercy Creek will provide the fresh beginning he has spent years imagining.

“A person should be judged by what he chooses to do next.”

Utah

Mary Farris

A former fitness instructor who taught spin classes and swimming lessons, Mary is energetic, perceptive, and quick to start a conversation. She intends to participate fully in island life and build friendships that feel genuine.

“You cannot begin again while standing still.”

California

Kevin Chen

The son of Chinese immigrants and a former technology professional, Kevin is precise, analytical, and intensely private. He enjoys systems, difficult problems, and work that rewards patience more than attention.

“Listen long enough and people explain themselves.”

Louisiana

Celestine Broussard

A former United States Marine and New Orleans police officer, Celestine brings discipline, life experience, and a calm presence to Mercy Creek. She values education, responsibility, and meaningful personal change.

“The years teach you what pride never could.”

Texas

Marco Reyes

A former varsity wrestler from Laredo, Marco is direct, competitive, and unwilling to reshape himself for anyone else's comfort. He values honesty and hopes Mercy Creek will offer a genuine chance to begin again.

“Tell the truth first. Deal with the rest afterward.”

Germany and North Carolina

Annika Brauer

Born in Berlin, Annika moved to the United States after marrying an American soldier. Thoughtful and quietly independent, she comes to Mercy Creek after personal loss, hoping a new environment might help her rediscover a future.

“Sometimes a good day surprises you.”

Ohio

Owen Marsh

Quiet and thoughtful, Owen spends more time with books than crowded rooms. Fascinated by history and classic literature, he often notices details others overlook and hopes Mercy Creek will help him step outside his comfort zone.

“Sometimes the quietest people notice the most.”

Originally from Thailand • Actuary

Pim Srisuwan

Pim came to the United States for college and built a successful career as an actuary. Analytical, patient, and quietly confident, she is accustomed to calculating risk but knows the people of Mercy Creek may be impossible to predict.

“You can calculate risk, but you cannot calculate people.”

Italian American

Paulo Rossi

Raised in a close-knit Italian family, Paulo loves cooking, gardening, and bringing people together over a good meal. Warm and easygoing, he believes conversation and food can turn strangers into friends.

“Food tastes better when nobody eats alone.”

Beyond the Island

The Audience Is Part of the Experiment

Families across the country watch the weekly program, study the residents, debate guilt and innocence, and decide whom they believe deserves another chance.

The viewers know the truth that Mercy Creek's residents do not. Every friendship and every act of trust is judged through that knowledge.

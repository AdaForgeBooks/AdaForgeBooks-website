Todd Thorne

Todd Thorne writes stories that explore forgiveness, survival, justice, faith, family and the choices that define ordinary people during extraordinary events.

Through Ada Forge Books, his work spans psychological horror, suspense, family drama, dystopian fiction and post-apocalyptic storytelling.

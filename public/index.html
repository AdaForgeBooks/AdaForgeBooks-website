About the Author

Todd Thorne

Todd Thorne writes fiction about survival, forgiveness, family, ambition, justice, faith, and the decisions that define ordinary people during extraordinary events.

Through Ada Forge Books, his work spans psychological horror, suspense, family drama, science fiction, and post-apocalyptic storytelling.
